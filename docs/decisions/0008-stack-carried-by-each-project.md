# Technologies are carried by each project, not by the landing

## Context and Problem Statement

The landing displayed a list of eight technologies. That is the reflex gesture of
a portfolio — and it says "look at everything I have mastered".

## Decision Drivers

- The writing rule: nothing is claimed, everything is inferred.
- `CONCEPT.md`: technologies serve as **evidence**, they are not the identity of
  the profile.

## Considered Options

- **On the landing** — a catalogue of mastery, unverifiable, and unrelated to
  what the landing has to do.
- **On the Profile chapter** — better, but still a catalogue.
- **In each project chapter** — "here is what I used for this system".

## Decision Outcome

Chosen option: "in each project chapter", under the introduction.

There the list becomes **judgeable**: it reads just before the trade-offs that
produced it. A lead or a CTO can assess choices, not a list of keywords.

### Consequences

- Good, because it is more modest and far more useful to the intended reader.
- Good, because `stack` becomes a property of `Project`, hence content like the
  rest.
- Bad, because the landing loses its lower-right mass. It was recomposed around
  the lede alone.
